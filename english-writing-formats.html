Master Written Pieces

1. Letter Formal & Informal

A letter is a written message sent to a person, organization, or authority to convey information, express opinions, or request action.

Key FeaturesFormal uses strict headers, sender/receiver addresses, subject, and formal tone. Informal uses relaxed, personal language.
When to UseFormal: Complaining about civic issues, applying for leave, writing to editors. Informal: Inviting friends, sharing personal news.

2. Notice Public Announcement

A brief, formal announcement used to inform a specific group of people about an upcoming event, rule change, or lost item.

Key FeaturesMust be enclosed within a **box**. Includes heading, organization name, date, title, and clear details (What, When, Where, Who) under ~50 words.
When to UseAnnouncing school cultural events, sports meets, debates, competitions, or notices about lost-and-found items.

3. Report Factual Account

An objective, factual description of an event, accident, or school activity that has already occurred, arranged in chronological order.

Key FeaturesIncludes a Headline and Byline. Written strictly in the **past tense** and third-person passive voice.
When to UseCovering school functions, annual day events, science exhibitions, workshops, or community disaster news.

4. Article Opinion & Analysis

A piece written for publication in a magazine or newspaper expressing thoughts, analysis, or arguments on a social issue or trend.

Key FeaturesCatchy Title and Byline. Structured into 3–4 paragraphs: Introduction, Body (causes/effects/arguments), and Conclusion with solutions.
When to UseDiscussing social issues (e.g., environmental awareness, digital addiction, mental health) for school magazines.

5. Email Digital Message

A quick digital communication format sent over the internet, following standard formal or semi-formal conventions in exams.

Key FeaturesHeader fields (To, Cc, Subject line), precise and direct body paragraphs, followed by a professional sign-off.
When to UseQuick official correspondence, inviting guest speakers, submitting assignments, or requesting project details.

6. Diary Entry Personal Reflection

A personal record where an individual notes down private thoughts, feelings, and emotional reflections about a specific day.

Key FeaturesStarts with Date, Day, and Time on the top-left. Written in the **first person ("I")** with an honest, expressive tone.
When to UseRecounting personal reactions to memorable events, winning prizes, dealing with failure, or visiting new places.

7. Slogan Catchy Phrase

An extremely short, catchy phrase designed to convey a powerful social message or promote an awareness campaign instantly.

Key FeaturesShort (3–10 words), rhythmic or rhyming, bold and visually striking layout.
When to UsePosters, rallies, public awareness drives (e.g., "Save Water, Save Life", "Say No to Plastic").
