Common Student Traps & How to Avoid Them

❌ Trap 1: The Binomial Square Distributive Illusion

Students often assume squaring a sum just squares individual terms.

❌ WRONG:
(a + b)² = a² + b²
✅ CORRECT:
(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

❌ Trap 2: Forgetting Alternating Signs in Cubes

When cubing a difference, signs alternate (+, -, +, -).

❌ WRONG:
(a - b)³ = a³ - 3a²b - 3ab² - b³
✅ CORRECT:
(a - b)³ = a³ - 3a²b + 3ab² - b³

❌ Trap 3: Sum of Cubes vs Binomial Cube Confusion

Factoring separate cubes is completely different from expanding a cubed binomial.

❌ WRONG:
a³ + b³ = (a + b)³
✅ CORRECT:
a³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² - ab + b²)

❌ Trap 4: Quadratic Discriminant Sign Error

The discriminant formula has a minus sign for 4ac.

❌ WRONG:
D = b² + 4ac
✅ CORRECT:
D = b² - 4ac
