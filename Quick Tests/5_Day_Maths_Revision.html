Day 1: Circle Theorems & Proofs High Priority

Target Questions: Q8, Q21(ii), Q22(ii), Q23(ii), Q33, Q34, Q40

1. Key Geometric Rules to Remember

  • Tangent-Radius Theorem: A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact (Angle = 90°).
  • Tangent Lengths: Tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal in length (PA = PB).
  • Angle in Semicircle: Angle subtended by a semicircle is always a Right Angle (90°).
  • Cyclic Quadrilateral: Opposite angles add up to 180°. Exterior angle equals interior opposite angle.
📐 Model Proof (Q40): Equal Tangents from External Point
Given a circle with center O and tangents PA, PB from external point P:
  1. In △OAP and △OBP: OA = OB (radii).
  2. OP = OP (common hypotenuse).
  3. ∠OAP = ∠OBP = 90° (radius ⊥ tangent).
  4. By RHS Congruence Criterion, △OAP ≅ △OBP ⟹ PA = PB (by CPCT).

Day 2: Mensuration & Units Calculation Focus

Target Questions: Q7, Q9, Q10, Q24(ii), Q25, Q41

1. Essential Formulas

  • Cylinder: Volume = πr²h, Curved Surface Area (CSA) = 2πrh.
  • Hemisphere: Volume = ⅔πr³, CSA = 2πr².
  • Circle: Circumference = 2πr, Area = πr².
⚠️ Caution - Unit Conversions & Multipliers (Q41):
If diameter is 40 cm ⟹ r = 20 cm. Height = 10 m = 1000 cm.
Always make sure units match! And if calculating for 14 pillars, multiply final volume by 14:

Total Volume = 14 × (²²/₇ × 20² × 1000) = 17,600,000 cm³

Day 3: Algebra & Word Problems

Target Questions: Q28(i-v), Q30, Q31

1. Boat Upstream/Downstream Framework (Q28)

Let speed of boat in still water = b km/h, speed of stream = x km/h.

  • Upstream Speed: (b - x) km/h
  • Downstream Speed: (b + x) km/h
  • Time Equation: Time = Distance / Speed

Day 4: Trigonometry & Coordinate Geometry

Target Questions: Q6, Q12, Q13, Q14, Q26, Q35

1. Key Trigonometric Identities & Triples

  • Pythagorean Triple (3, 4, 5): If tan A = ⁴/₃, Hypotenuse = 5 ⟹ cos A = ³/₅.
  • Complementary Angles: tan(2A) = cot(90° - 2A) ⟹ 90° - 2A = A - 18° ⟹ A = 36°.

2. Distance from Axes

For point P(x, y): Distance from y-axis = |x|; Distance from x-axis = |y|.

Day 5: Statistics & Arithmetic Progression

Target Questions: Q3, Q19, Q27, Q36, Q37, Q38

1. Core Rules

  • Median Shift Property: If every raw data value is increased by k, the new median = Old Median + k.
  • Sum of Odd Numbers: Sum of first n odd natural numbers = n². (e.g., first 20 odd numbers = 20² = 400).
  • Median of Frequency Table: Construct Cumulative Frequency (cf) column, find N/2 position, locate corresponding xᵢ.
